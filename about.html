Company Heritage

Born In The Shipyard, Proven On The Water

Brothers Machine Services Corp was founded on a simple, non-negotiable principle: Discipline and Results. In the maritime industry, there are no shortcuts. When a commercial vessel or naval craft is docked, every hour in the slipway represents thousands of dollars in operating costs.

Rather than relying on desk-bound engineering or outsourcing critical cuts to third parties, our founders built this company with their hands on the dials, indicators, and heavy boring bars.

Through decades of dedicated service at GMD Navy Shipyard (Brooklyn Navy Yard) and Caddell Drydock (Staten Island), Brothers Machine has earned the unconditional trust of port engineers, tug fleet superintendents, and naval ship repairers.

"Outside machining isn't just a trade—it’s an exacting craft where physics, maritime pressures, and thousandths of an inch meet under tight dock schedules."

Chronicle of Growth

Company History & Milestones

A timeline of dedication, expanding capabilities, and trusted shipyard partnerships.

Foundation & Roots

Master Craftsmen Unite

Founded by master marine machinists with a vision to eliminate unnecessary vessel teardowns. Starting with custom-built portable line boring rigs, the team immediately began servicing regional tug and commercial workboat fleets in NY/NJ harbor waters.

Strategic Integration

Stationed at Caddell Drydock & GMD Navy Yard

Formally establishing continuous outside machining operations inside Caddell Drydock on Staten Island and GMD Shipyard in Brooklyn Navy Yard. Entrusted with large-scale stern tube line boring, rudder refurbishment, and heavy deck machinery reconditioning.

Engineering Expansion

Optical Alignment & Heavy Portable Tooling

Acquisition of digital laser collimators, long-span 40-foot boring bars, and hydraulic bolt tensioning equipment. Enabling simultaneous multi-bearing stern tube line boring and precision propulsion alignments verified directly to ABS and USCG standards.

Present & Beyond

Harbor-Wide Leader in Outside Machining

Operating with comprehensive marine insurance policies (Ship Repairer’s Legal Liability & Marine GL), Brothers Machine Services Corp stands as the premier on-site machining specialist for shipyards, naval architects, and vessel operators across the Eastern Seaboard.
