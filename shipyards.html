Brooklyn, New York

GMD Shipyard (Brooklyn Navy Yard)

Located within the historic Brooklyn Navy Yard on the East River, GMD Shipyard is the largest full-service ship repair facility in New York City. Operating Dry Docks 1, 5, and 6, GMD accommodates deep-draft commercial ships, Military Sealift Command vessels, articulated tug-barges (ATBs), and cruise passenger ferries.

Brothers Machine Services Corp acts as an essential outside machining partner at GMD:

⚓
Graving Dock 1 & 6 Support: Executing large-bore line boring for deep-draft commercial vessels and state ferries.
⚓
Propulsion Shaft Line Boring: Continuous optical and laser alignment from stern tube to engine bedplate.
⚓
Class Society Certifications: Coordinating directly with ABS, USCG, and DNV surveyors on fit tolerances.

Staten Island, New York

Caddell Drydock and Repair Co.

Situated on the Kill Van Kull in Staten Island, Caddell Drydock is an icon of East Coast marine repair with over a century of continuous service. Featuring 6 floating drydocks with lifting capacities up to 7,300 tons, Caddell is the primary hub for New York Harbor’s tug fleets, petroleum barges, dredges, and municipal ferries.

Brothers Machine Services Corp provides fast-response outside machining across all floating docks:

⚓
Tugboat & Workboat Rapid Turnaround: In-place rudder trunk boring and pintle sleeve machining to keep tugs working.
⚓
Deck Machinery & Towing Winches: Overhauling massive towing winches, capstans, and brake drums on deck.
⚓
Strut & Cutless Bearing Line Boring: Rapid restoration of worn strut bores on multi-screw vessels.
