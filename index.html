The Concept of Outside Machining

We Bring Precision Tooling Directly to the Vessel

In naval and heavy industrial operations, dismantling a propeller shaft, stern tube, rudder stock, or engine block for conventional offsite machining means catastrophic delays, exorbitant crane fees, and extended drydock demurrage.

Outside Machining is the rigorous discipline of mounting heavy, high-torque portable machine tools directly onto the vessel structure. Whether suspended in a drydock slipway or confined inside a ship’s shaft alley, our seasoned machinists restore true concentricity and factory finishes without pulling major structural components.

✔
Zero Dismantling Demurrage: Eliminate weeks of vessel downtime by machining critical bores, flanges, and shafts in place.
✔
Harbor-Wide Portability: Rapid mobilization to any drydock, slipway, pier, or offshore anchorage across NY/NJ and beyond.
✔
Certified Optical & Laser Alignment: Perfect optical centerline verification before, during, and after every cut.

Working inside shipyards, aboard commercial vessels, and across navigable waters demands specialized, comprehensive insurance coverage. Standard commercial general liability is not enough for drydock operations.

Marine General Liability (MGL): Broad form waterborne and drydock general liability coverage.
USL&H & Maritime Workers’ Compensation: Full United States Longshore and Harbor Workers’ Compensation coverage for all field technicians.
